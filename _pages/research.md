My research explores how magnetic fields, turbulence, and waves interact inside stars. I combine numerical simulations, magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), and helioseismology to investigate physical processes that are otherwise inaccessible to direct observation.

This page introduces the main themes of my research through a series of scientific stories. Each story begins with a scientific question and follows the path through numerical experiments, analysis, and discovery.
